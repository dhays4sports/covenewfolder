# CF-HOME-2.8 — Continuity, Branching & Recovery

CoverageFit v3.20.59 closes two bounded navigation gaps around the established Home assessment.

When a trusted 408FARMERS prospect profile remains in the browser but the short-lived transition marker is missing, the transition identifies the handoff as recovered and continues to the existing assessment. If a recent trusted renter handoff reaches the homeowner transition or assessment by mistake, CoverageFit returns it to the 408FARMERS renters contact path with no personal data in the URL.

Owner-occupied, landlord, and buyer entries continue through the same Home assessment. The existing seven-day assessment draft, one-time property confirmation, zero-repeat contact and consent reuse, questions, recommendations, completed record, and Protection Score remain unchanged.
